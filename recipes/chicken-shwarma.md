# Chicken Shawarma
A bright, spiced roast chicken with onions and plenty of serving possibilities.

## info  
* About 1 hour 15 minutes  
* 4 to 6 servings  

## ingredients
* 2 lemons, juiced
* 1/2 cup plus 1 tablespoon olive oil
* 6 cloves garlic, peeled, smashed, and minced
* 1 teaspoon kosher salt
* 2 teaspoons freshly ground black pepper
* 2 teaspoons ground cumin
* 2 teaspoons paprika
* 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
* A pinch ground cinnamon
* Crushed red pepper, to taste
* 2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs
* 1 large red onion, peeled and quartered
* 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley

## steps  
1. Combine the lemon juice, 1/2 cup olive oil, garlic, salt, pepper, cumin, paprika, turmeric, cinnamon, and crushed red pepper in a large bowl and whisk to combine. Add the chicken and toss well to coat. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour and up to 12 hours.
1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Use the remaining tablespoon of olive oil to grease a rimmed sheet pan. Add the quartered onion to the chicken and marinade, and toss once to combine. Remove the chicken and onion from the marinade and place them on the pan, spreading everything evenly across it.
1. Roast until the chicken is browned, crisp at the edges, and cooked through, about 30 to 40 minutes. Remove from the oven and let rest for 2 minutes, then slice into pieces.
1. Scatter the parsley over the top and serve with tomatoes, cucumbers, pita, white sauce, hot sauce, olives, fried eggplant, feta, rice, or your favorite accompaniments.

## notes  
* For extra crisp edges, sear the sliced chicken in a hot pan with a little olive oil until it curls and browns.
* The chicken is flavorful enough to serve as a wrap, bowl, or platter.
