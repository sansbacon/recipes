# Curry-Spiced Whole Roasted Cauliflower
A whole roasted cauliflower with a warm curry butter, toasted nuts, and a bright yogurt-tahini sauce.

## info  
* About 1 hour 15 minutes  
* 4 servings  

## ingredients
* 3 ounces butter
* 3 tablespoons olive oil
* 1 tablespoon chopped garlic
* 1 teaspoon curry powder
* 1 head cauliflower
* Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
* 1/2 cup almonds, chopped
* 1/2 cup golden raisins
* 1 lime, zested and juiced
* 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
* 2 tablespoons tahini
* 1/4 cup dill, minced
* 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
* 1 clove garlic, minced
* Juice of 1 lemon
* 1 cup cilantro leaves
* 1 cup mint leaves
* 1 Fresno chile, sliced

## steps  
1. Preheat the oven to 450°F.
1. Heat the butter and olive oil in a small saucepan over medium heat until the butter melts. Add the chopped garlic and curry powder and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
1. Make a small foil ring wide enough to hold up the base of the cauliflower a little. Place the foil ring in a large sauté pan and set the cauliflower on top. Season with salt and pepper. Pour all but 1/4 cup of the curry butter over the cauliflower.
1. Roast the cauliflower for 10 minutes, then baste with some of the curry butter from the bottom of the pan. Continue roasting and basting until the cauliflower is tender, about 45 minutes total.
1. Meanwhile, add the almonds and raisins to the remaining curry butter in the saucepan and cook over medium heat until the nuts are toasted and the raisins are plump, about 3 minutes. Stir in the lime juice.
1. Remove the cauliflower to a serving platter and drizzle with any remaining butter from the pan. Top with the nut, raisin, and curry butter mixture and sprinkle with the lime zest.
1. For the sauce, whisk together the Greek yogurt, olive oil, tahini, dill, cumin, minced garlic, and lemon juice in a bowl. Season with salt and pepper.
1. Drizzle the cauliflower with the yogurt-tahini sauce and garnish with cilantro, mint, and sliced Fresno chile.

## notes  
* A foil ring helps the cauliflower stand upright and roast more evenly.
* The sauce can be made ahead and chilled until serving.
