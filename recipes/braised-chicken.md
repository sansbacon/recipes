# Braised Chicken Thighs
Basic recipe for braising chicken thighs.

## info  
* About 40 minutes  
* 12-16 servings  

## ingredients
* 1 cup chicken broth
* 1 tablespoon sugar
* 1 tablespoon ground cumin
* 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
* 3 cloves garlic, skin removed and smashed
* Salt and pepper
* 4 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs, halved crosswise

## steps  
1. Bring broth, sugar, spices, salt, pepper, and garlic to boil in large Dutch oven over high heat, stirring to dissolve sugar. 
1. Add chicken and return to simmer. Reduce heat to medium-low.
1. Cover, and cook, stirring occasionally, until chicken is easily shredded with fork, about 25 minutes.
1. Using tongs, transfer chicken to medium bowl and set aside. 
1. Strain cooking liquid through fine-mesh strainer set over bowl. 
1. Let liquid settle for 5 minutes; skim fat from surface. 
1. Using tongs, squeeze chicken until shredded into bite-size pieces. 
1. Add 3 tablespoons of the defatted liquid to the shredded chicken.

## notes  
* This is adapted from the Cook's Illustrated recipe for pulled chicken.
