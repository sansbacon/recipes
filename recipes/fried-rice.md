# Fried Rice
A quick, savory skillet rice with scrambled egg, vegetables, and toasted grains.

## info
* 20 minutes
* 2 servings

## ingredients
* 2 cups cooked white rice (350g), preferably day-old
* 2 tablespoons vegetable or canola oil, divided
* 1 small onion, finely chopped (115g)
* 1 medium carrot, small dice (85g)
* 2 scallions, thinly sliced (30g)
* 2 cloves garlic, minced (5g)
* 1 teaspoon soy sauce
* 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
* 1 large egg
* 115g frozen peas
* Kosher salt, to taste
* Ground white pepper, to taste

## steps
1. If using day-old rice, break up any clumps into individual grains.
1. Heat 1/2 tablespoon oil in a wok over high heat until smoking. Add half the rice and stir-fry until lightly toasted and pale brown, about 3 minutes. Transfer to a bowl. Repeat with another 1/2 tablespoon oil and remaining rice.
1. Return all rice to the wok and push it up the sides. Add 1/2 tablespoon oil to the center. Add onion, carrot, scallions, and garlic. Stir-fry for 1 minute until fragrant and slightly softened. Mix vegetables into the rice.
1. Add soy sauce and sesame oil. Toss well to coat. Season with kosher salt and white pepper to taste.
1. Push rice to one side of the wok. Add remaining 1/2 tablespoon oil. Crack in the egg and lightly season with salt. Scramble into small pieces. Mix egg into the rice.
1. Add frozen peas. Stir-fry until peas are heated through and rice grains are fully separated. Serve immediately.

## notes
* Day-old rice produces the best texture and helps prevent clumping.
* High heat is important for achieving lightly toasted grains and wok hei flavor.
