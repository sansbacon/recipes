# Sous-Vide Chicken Breast
A simple, juicy chicken breast with a soy-brined flavor and a quick pan-sear finish.

## info  
* About 8 hours 30 minutes  
* 1 serving  

## ingredients
* 1 chicken breast
* 2 quarts (2 L) water
* 1/2 cup (130 g) soy sauce
* 1/8 cup (30 g) brown sugar
* 30 g kosher salt
* 4 garlic cloves, crushed
* 4 sprigs thyme
* 2 bay leaves
* 1 lemon, quartered
* 1 sprig rosemary
* 1 bunch parsley
* 2 teaspoons (5 g) black peppercorn
* 1 tablespoon (15 g) vegetable oil
* 1 tablespoon (15 g) kosher salt
* 1 teaspoon (3 g) fresh cracked black pepper
* 1 teaspoon (4 g) garlic powder
* 1/2 teaspoon (2 g) onion powder
* 1 teaspoon (5 g) MSG

## steps  
1. In a 4-quart pot or storage container, whisk together the water, soy sauce, brown sugar, salt, garlic, thyme, rosemary, parsley, and black peppercorn until the salt dissolves.
1. Add the chicken breast to the brine, cover, and refrigerate for 6 to 8 hours.
1. After brining, remove the chicken from the brine and pat it dry with a paper towel.
1. Preheat the sous vide to 66°C.
1. In a small bowl, whisk together the vegetable oil, salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and MSG until fully incorporated.
1. Put the chicken breast in a vacuum-sealed bag with the seasoning mixture, seal, and cook in the sous vide for 60 minutes.
1. Remove the chicken from the bag and pat it dry again.
1. Preheat a cast iron pan over medium-high heat, then add a little vegetable oil until it begins to smoke.
1. Season the chicken lightly with salt and pepper, then sear for 2 to 3 minutes per side until nicely browned.

## notes  
* The brine makes the chicken especially savory, so keep the sear quick and high heat.
* If you do not have a vacuum sealer, a zip-top bag with the air pressed out works well.
