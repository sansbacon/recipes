# Chicken and Dumplings
A cozy chicken stew with buttery dumplings and plenty of vegetables.

## info  
* About 1 hour 30 minutes  
* 6 servings  

## ingredients
* 2 pounds bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
* Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
* 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
* 1 large yellow onion, diced
* 4 stalks celery, chopped
* 6 medium carrots (about 1 1/2 pounds), peeled and sliced 1/4-inch thick
* 4 sprigs thyme
* 1 tablespoon unsalted butter, plus more as needed
* 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
* 2 large leeks, white and light green parts thinly sliced
* 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
* 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
* 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt
* 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground pepper
* 3/4 cup buttermilk
* 1 large egg
* 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
* 1/2 cup parsley, tender leaves and stems, finely chopped (optional)
* 1/4 cup chives, finely chopped (optional)

## steps  
1. Season the chicken on both sides with salt and pepper. Heat the oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Working in batches if needed, sear the chicken, skin-side down, until deeply golden brown, 8 to 10 minutes. Flip and continue cooking until browned on the other side, another 5 to 8 minutes. Transfer the chicken to a plate and pour off all the fat into a measuring cup. You should have about 5 tablespoons, depending on the fattiness of the chicken.
1. Leaving the browned bits in the pot, return 2 tablespoons of fat to the pot. Add the onions, celery, and half the carrots. Season with salt and pepper and cook over medium heat, stirring to scrape up the browned bits on the bottom of the pot. Cook until the vegetables start to soften, about 4 minutes. Return the chicken to the pot along with the thyme and 8 cups of water. Simmer, uncovered, until the chicken is completely tender and the liquid has reduced by about 1/4, about 30 to 40 minutes.
1. Transfer the chicken to a plate or cutting board to cool. Strain the stock, which should yield about 5 cups; if you have less, add water to make up the difference. Wipe out the pot.
1. Heat the remaining 3 tablespoons of chicken fat along with 1 tablespoon butter (or enough butter to equal 4 tablespoons of fat if you do not have enough chicken fat) over medium heat. Sprinkle in the flour and stir constantly until pale golden brown, about 2 to 3 minutes.
1. Slowly whisk in the reserved chicken stock until no lumps remain. It will thicken considerably at first. Bring to a boil. Add the leeks and remaining carrots, season with salt and pepper, and lower the heat to a simmer.
1. Remove and discard the skin and bones from the chicken. Shred the meat and add it to the pot. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the liquid is nicely thickened and the carrots and leeks are tender, 10 to 12 minutes.
1. Make the dumplings: In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt, and pepper. In a small bowl, whisk together the buttermilk and egg and add to the dry ingredients, followed by the melted butter. Stir just to combine; do not overmix.
1. Using a spoon, drop generous quarter-size dollops of the dumpling dough into the pot so they sit on top, spacing them apart as much as possible. Cover the pot and reduce the heat to low. Cook, undisturbed, until the dumplings are puffed and cooked through, 18 to 22 minutes. If needed, test one by cutting it in half; it should be slightly biscuity with no raw dough.
1. Remove the lid and divide the stew among bowls. Sprinkle with parsley and chives, if using.

## notes  
* The stew is especially good made a day ahead; the flavors deepen overnight.
* Keep the dumpling dough lightly mixed so the dumplings stay tender.
