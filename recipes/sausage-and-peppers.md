# Grilled Italian Sausage With Sweet-and-Sour Peppers and Onions
A smoky, sweet-and-sour grill dinner with juicy sausages and caramelized peppers.

## info  
* About 1 hour 10 minutes  
* 4 servings  

## ingredients
* 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
* 1 large onion, sliced into 1/4-inch strips
* 2 red bell peppers, stems and cores removed, sliced into 1/4-inch strips
* 2 green bell peppers, stems and cores removed, sliced into 1/4-inch strips
* 3 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
* 3 tablespoons sugar
* Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
* 2 pounds sweet or hot Italian sausage
* Buns, for serving

## steps  
1. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add the onions and peppers and cook, stirring often, until softened and browned in spots, about 8 minutes.
1. Add the vinegar and sugar, then remove from the heat and stir until the sugar dissolves. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
1. Transfer the pepper mixture to a disposable aluminum pan or a double-layer foil tray. Nestle the sausages into the pan.
1. Place the pan on the hot side of the grill and cook until simmering, about 4 minutes. Slide it to the cooler side of the grill, cover, and cook until the sausages register 140 to 145°F, about 20 minutes, turning once halfway through.
1. Remove the sausages from the peppers and place them directly on the grill grate over the hot side. Cook, turning occasionally, until well browned and crisp, about 3 minutes total. Return them to the peppers and serve with buns.

## notes  
* The peppers and onions can be made ahead and reheated gently before finishing the sausages on the grill.
* For extra sweetness, use sweet Italian sausage; for more heat, use hot sausage.
